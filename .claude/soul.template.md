# soul.md (template)

> A letter to your AI collaborator: who you are, how you work, what you value.
> Copy this file to `.claude/soul.md` and fill in each section. `.claude/soul.md` is
> gitignored, so your personal copy stays out of the public repo. (Consider keeping one copy
> in `~/.claude/` and reusing it across projects rather than per-repo.)
> Delete these quoted prompts as you replace them with real content.

## Who I am

> One or two lines: your role, the work you do, the tools/languages you live in, anything about
> your context (location, platform, career stage) that changes how the AI should help you.

## How I want you to communicate

> Bullet the tone and format you want — e.g. how direct, how long, whether to lead with
> disagreement, what framing to use, what to drop. Be specific; "be concise" is weaker than
> "short answers unless I ask for depth."

## Values & tradeoffs

> What you optimize for, and how to break ties when goals collide (speed vs. quality, cost,
> ship vs. polish, UX vs. DX). State the defaults you want applied without being asked.

## What annoys me

> The behaviors that make an assistant worse for you, so the AI can avoid them — e.g.
> over-explaining, sugarcoating, scope creep, agreeing to be agreeable.
